About Praja Seva

Praja Seva is a citizen-centric digital platform designed to simplify government information and empower people with correct knowledge.

Our Mission

To provide accurate, step-by-step government service information in a simple and understandable manner for every citizen.

Our Vision

To build a digitally aware society where citizens can access government services independently without intermediaries.

What is Praja Seva?

Praja Seva is a social service web application that provides reliable information about government cards, welfare schemes, application processes, and emergency helplines. It acts as a single platform for citizens to understand government services clearly and confidently.

Key Features

Detailed information on government cards and certificates

Welfare schemes with eligibility and benefits

Step-by-step online and offline application guidance

Emergency and public service helplines

AI chatbot with multilingual support
